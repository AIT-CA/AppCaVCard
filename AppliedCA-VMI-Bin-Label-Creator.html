Printing on the LabelWriter
  1. Open the saved PDF and choose Print.
  2. Pick the DYMO LabelWriter 550 Turbo.
  3. Set paper size to 30336 1 in x 2-1/8 in.
  4. Set scale to 100% / Actual size, not Fit to page.
  5. Print one label and scan it with your phone before printing the batch.

Word file: each label is one page sized 2-1/8 x 1 in. Open it in Word, choose Print, pick the LabelWriter and the 30336 paper size. Text and QR codes can be edited there.
